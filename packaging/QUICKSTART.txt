ARW (Army Retirement Workbench)
================================

1. Open ARW:
     Mac:     double-click ARW.app. The first time, right-click it,
              choose Open, then choose Open again.
     Windows: double-click arw.exe. If a blue SmartScreen box appears,
              choose More info, then Run anyway.
     Linux:   run ./arw in a terminal.

2. ARW opens in your web browser at http://127.0.0.1:5252.
   If it does not, type that address into your browser.

3. Go to Settings. Enter your name, branch, and retirement date, then
   click Save. Click Generate My Timeline.

4. To stop ARW, go to Settings and click Quit ARW.
   Your data is saved on your computer the whole time.

5. Back up often: Settings > Back Up Everything. Keep the zip on a USB drive.

Use a personal computer, not a government computer.
This is a planning tool, not official guidance. Confirm every date and form
with your transition counselor, your unit S-1, and an accredited VSO.
