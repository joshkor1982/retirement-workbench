
## Install

1. Download the file for your computer below: **macOS.zip** for any Mac, **windows-x64.zip** for most PCs, **windows-arm64.zip** for Windows on ARM, or a **linux** tar.gz.
2. Unzip it and open **ARW**. It opens in your browser at `http://127.0.0.1:5252`.
3. The first launch shows a warning because the app is not signed yet. On a Mac, right-click **ARW.app**, choose **Open**, then **Open** again. On Windows, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Use a personal computer, not a government computer. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lets you check your download.

This is a beta: back up often (**Settings > Back Up Everything**) and [report problems](https://github.com/joshkor1982/retirement-workbench/issues/new/choose) without any personal information.
