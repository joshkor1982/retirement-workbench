Estimated Rating

How This Works

The Advisor reads your DBQs and medical records, plus the condition notes and symptom log, and rates each condition under 38 CFR Part 4 the way a C&P examiner would. The combined percentage is computed with the VA's own combined-ratings math and bilateral factor, then rounded to the nearest ten. It is a planning estimate, not a decision - an accredited VSO files the claim. Re-run it after you upload new evidence.

Conditions for the Claim

How to Use

One row per condition you intend to claim. Documented means the condition appears in your medical record; DBQ means you studied the questionnaire for it before the exams. Both need to be true before the BDD claim files. The claim is built from the record, so keep the symptom log below current and get every condition into the record at the retirement physical. Find Conditions in My Records has the Advisor read your uploaded records and DBQs and suggest conditions they document that you have not listed.
