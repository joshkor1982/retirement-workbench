Neighborhood Map

How to Use

Switch layers on to see what matters around a house: FEMA flood zones, population density from the 2020 Census, public schools, crime rates from the FBI, and places from OpenStreetMap like grocery stores, hospitals, parks, and gyms. Places and schools load for the area in view, so zoom in to a neighborhood. Click the map to zoom with the scroll wheel, and click any dot for details. Crime is reported per police department or sheriff's office, so it describes the whole city or county, not one street; a free api.data.gov key in Settings keeps it from hitting the demo limit.

Flood Zones Shaded areas are FEMA special flood hazard areas (1% annual chance, the "100-year" flood); lighter shading is the 0.2% area. A lender will require flood insurance in the 1% zones. FEMA draws them only when zoomed in to street level. Confirm a specific address at FEMA's Flood Map Service Center ↗.
