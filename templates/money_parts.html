Retired Pay Estimate

How This Works

Your pension is your years of service times 2.5% (High-3) or 2.0% (Blended Retirement System), times the average of your highest 36 months of base pay. SBP for full spouse coverage costs 6.5% of it. VA compensation is tax-free: at a combined rating of 50% or more you receive both in full (CRDP); below 50%, VA pay replaces an equal amount of retired pay. The rating comes from your Potential VA Rating. This is a planning estimate before taxes, not a DFAS statement.

Debt Avalanche

How to Use

Highest APR dies first. Pay the Power Payment (the engine plus the target's minimum) at the top of the list, exact minimums on everything else, and when a card hits zero, roll its whole payment into the next one. Operational freeze: no new charges; emergencies come from the emergency fund. Windfalls (tax refund, the recovered held pay, raises) surge the current target.
